PORTION SIZE RULES (grams):
  protein/fish source: 100–200g
  vegetables:          50–150g
  grains/legumes:      60–150g cooked weight
  oils/dressings:      5–15g

  nuts/seeds:          15–30g
  spices:              1–5g
  dairy (yogurt):      100–200g

NUTRIENT CALCULATION:
  total_X = sum(food.X * portion_g / 100)
  for all ingredients in meal

ANTI-INFLAMMATORY SCORE:
  weighted average of ingredient scores
  weight = portion_g / sum(all portion_g)
  clamp 0–10, round to 1 decimal

TAG RULES (auto-assign):
  high_protein      → total_protein_g >= 25
