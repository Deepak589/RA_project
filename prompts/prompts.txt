PROJECT CONTEXT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

App purpose:
- Help adults with Rheumatoid Arthritis log meals and symptoms
- Recommend the next best meal based on nutrition rules
- Track daily lifestyle factors (sleep, activity, stress)
- Later (V2/V3): detect flare triggers and predict pain/flare risk

Target user: Adult with RA who wants practical food and lifestyle guidance
Medical boundary: Supportive lifestyle tool ONLY — not a diagnostic system,
not a replacement for a rheumatologist or dietitian

Tech stack:
- Backend: FastAPI (Python)
- Database: PostgreSQL
- Frontend: React + Vite (built in later weeks)
- ML: scikit-learn / XGBoost (added in V3, not now)
